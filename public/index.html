Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Praxis SA ("Praxis SA", "the platform", "we", "us", "our") is operated by Greg Laxton as a sole proprietor. We are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who We Are

Praxis SA is a technology platform that facilitates introductions between homeowners and independent trade professionals in South Africa, and provides workspaces for landlords (Landlord Pro) and building contractors (Construction Pro) to manage their properties and projects. We are not an employer of, nor do we control, any professional listed on the platform. We are not a party to any agreement between users, or between users and their tenants or clients.

For all privacy-related matters, contact us at: greg@laxtonsa.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the platform:

  • Name, email address, and phone number when you register
  • Location data (zip code, suburb, city) to match you with nearby professionals
  • For professionals: identity documents, police clearance certificates, and trade qualifications submitted for verification purposes
  • Job details, quotes, messages, and reviews you create through the platform
  • Banking details provided by professionals, used solely for the purpose of platform fee invoicing and payment processing
  • For landlords: property details, tenant names and contact details, lease dates, maintenance tickets, and rental expense records with receipts
  • For builders: project details (site address, client name and contact details, contract value), stages and payment claims, variation orders, site diary entries, photos, and subcontractor details
  • Information submitted by tenants and building clients through private links, described in section 6
  • Your subscription plan and its status, as reported by the App Store or Google Play
  • Device information and app usage data collected automatically for platform stability purposes

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To connect homeowners with appropriate trade professionals
  • To verify the identity and credentials of professionals on the platform
  • To process platform fees and generate invoices
  • To let landlords receive and act on their tenants' maintenance reports, and keep their rental expense records
  • To let builders administer their projects, share progress with their clients, and record their clients' answers to variation orders
  • To manage paid subscriptions
  • To send transactional communications (quote notifications, payment confirmations, account alerts)
  • To improve the platform and resolve technical issues
  • To comply with applicable South African law

We do not use your personal information for marketing to third parties, and we do not sell your data under any circumstances.

4. Document Verification & Storage

Identity documents and certificates submitted by professionals for verification are stored in private, encrypted cloud storage (Supabase) accessible only to the platform operator. Documents are reviewed solely for the purpose of confirming identity and credentials. We retain only a verified status indicator and the date of verification. Original document files are permanently deleted once verification is complete. Documents are never shared with third parties, including homeowners.

5. Location Data

We use your zip code or suburb to calculate approximate distances between homeowners and professionals. We do not track your real-time GPS location, and no location data is shared with third parties.

6. Tenants and Building Clients Using Private Links

Some people use Praxis SA without creating an account, through a private link sent to them by a registered account holder:

  • Tenants. A landlord on Landlord Pro can send each tenant a private link (praxis-sa.net/t/…, which opens the tenant portal at praxis-sa.net/tenant). Through it, a tenant reports maintenance issues: a description of the fault, its urgency, the trade needed, and site photos. Reports are linked to the name and contact details the landlord already holds for that tenant. The link shows the tenant only their landlord's first name and the suburb and city of the property.
  • Building clients. A builder on Construction Pro can send their client a private link to their project (praxis-sa.net/p/…, which opens the project portal at praxis-sa.net/project). Through it, the client views the project's progress, its stages, approved changes, and the site diary entries the builder has chosen to share, and approves or declines variation orders. A client can add a written note to an approval, and must add one to decline. We record the answer, the note, and when it was given. The link never shows payment claims, retention, or the builder's subcontractors.

Purpose and lawful basis. Information submitted through these links is processed only to facilitate property maintenance, record-keeping, and contract administration on behalf of the landlord or builder who sent the link. This processing is necessary for the account holder's agreement with the tenant or client (the lease or building contract) and for their legitimate interest in maintaining the property and administering the contract, as allowed by section 11 of POPIA.

Who is responsible. For this information, the registered landlord or builder is the Responsible Party: they decide to collect it, invite the tenant or client, and use the records. Praxis SA acts as their Operator, processing the information on their behalf to deliver the platform service, and does not use it for any other purpose.

When links stop working. A link stops working as soon as the account holder replaces it with a new one or deactivates it. A tenant's link also stops 30 days after their lease ends. A project link also stops if the project is cancelled. Both stop while the account holder's plan has lapsed.

7. Photos and Site Records

Maintenance and construction photos, including those submitted through private links, are stored in private cloud storage and are never publicly listed. Each is accessible only to:

  • the account holder (landlord or builder) whose property or project it belongs to
  • for maintenance tickets, the tradesman on Praxis SA the landlord has sent the ticket to
  • for construction projects, the holder of the project's private link, who sees only the photos on variation orders sent to them and on diary entries the builder has shared

Photos shown through a private link are delivered as signed links that expire within an hour.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information only as strictly necessary to operate the platform:

  • Supabase — our database and storage provider, operating under appropriate data processing agreements
  • Resend — our transactional email provider, used only to deliver account and job-related notifications
  • Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat — to process and manage paid subscriptions. Payment card details are handled by Apple or Google and never reach Praxis SA
  • Other users, as the platform works — for example, a maintenance ticket and its photos reach the tradesman a landlord sends it to, and a tenant's contact details are shared with that tradesman only if the landlord chooses to share them
  • Law enforcement or regulatory authorities — only where required by South African law or a valid court order

No other third parties receive your personal information.

9. Data Security

All data is stored on encrypted servers. Each account's records are accessible only to that account holder, the platform operator, and the people the account holder shares them with through the platform. Private links contain a long random key that can't be guessed, and they aren't indexed by search engines. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

10. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we will permanently remove your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (for example, financial records). Verification documents are deleted immediately upon completion of the verification process.

Property and project records that support financial and tax records, including invoices, certificates, expense ledgers with their receipts, payment claims, and site logs, are retained in line with South African statutory requirements. For example, SARS generally requires records supporting a tax return to be kept for five years. For this reason, a property with expense records, or a project with payment claims on record, can't be deleted from the app until those requirements have been met.

Tenants and building clients who don't have an account can ask for their information to be corrected or removed by emailing greg@laxtonsa.com, or through the landlord or builder who sent them their link. Where the information forms part of the account holder's statutory records, we will tell you what has to be kept and why.

11. Your Rights Under POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

These rights apply equally to tenants and building clients who use a private link without an account. They can contact us directly, or the landlord or builder who sent them the link.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at greg@laxtonsa.com. We will respond within 30 days. To request account and data deletion, click below:

13. Children

Praxis SA is not directed at persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, contact us immediately at greg@laxtonsa.com.
