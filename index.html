Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

FixIt SA (Pty) Ltd ("FixIt SA", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, email address, and phone number when you register
  • Location data (zip code, suburb, city) to match you with nearby professionals
  • For professionals: identity documents, police clearance certificates, and trade qualifications submitted for verification
  • Job details, quotes, messages, and reviews created through the platform
  • Banking details for professionals (used solely for invoicing and platform fee payments)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To connect homeowners with appropriate trade professionals
  • To verify the identity and credentials of professionals on the platform
  • To process platform fees and generate invoices
  • To send transactional emails (quote notifications, payment confirmations)

3. Document Verification & POPIA Compliance

Identity documents and certificates submitted for verification are stored temporarily in private, encrypted storage. Documents are reviewed by FixIt SA staff only. Once verification is complete, the original document files are permanently deleted. We retain only a verified status (true/false) and the date of verification. Documents are never shared with third parties.

4. Location Data

We use your zip code to calculate distances between homeowners and professionals. We do not track your real-time GPS location.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited information is shared only as necessary to operate the platform. We use Supabase for secure data storage and Resend for transactional emails.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting privacy@fixitsa.co.za.

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Contact us at privacy@fixitsa.co.za. We will respond within 30 days.
